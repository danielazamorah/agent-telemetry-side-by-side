5 · Why two dictionaries exist

OpenTelemetry GenAI conventions

Who: the OpenTelemetry project (GenAI SIG), vendor neutral.

For: any observability backend: Cloud Trace, Datadog, Grafana.

Style: gen_ai.*, numbers on spans, words as opt-in events, privacy first.

Status: still marked Development in the spec, so names still move.

OpenInference

Who: Arize and the community, open source.

For: LLM debugging and evaluation tools (Phoenix, Arize, eval pipelines).

Style: span kinds (AGENT, LLM, TOOL, CHAIN, RETRIEVER), words on the span, one place to read everything.

Status: stable, widely used, instrumentors for many frameworks.

The framework doesn't decide the dictionary, the instrumentor does: the same ADK agent with the OpenInference instrumentor writes OpenInference (see traces/adk-openinference__*.json). So, pick the dictionary your tools read, or normalize both at rest with SQL.
